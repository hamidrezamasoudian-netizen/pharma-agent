# Sources used for topic discovery

- Google Trends Iran: https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?geo=IR
- Tebna RSS directory: https://www.tebna.ir/rss-help
  - Health and treatment: https://www.tebna.ir/rss/tp/2
  - Drug guide: https://www.tebna.ir/rss/tp/83
- Salamat News RSS directory: https://www.salamatnews.com/rss-help
  - Most viewed health news: https://www.salamatnews.com/rss/pl/241
  - Drugs and supplements: https://www.salamatnews.com/rss/pl/233

The repository uses these public RSS sources as configurable defaults, with topic normalization, similarity checks, and a 180-day history to reduce repeated subjects. Google Trends provides Iran search-trend exploration; the publisher pages document the listed RSS endpoints.
